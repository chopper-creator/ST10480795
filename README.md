Tech Bridge Youth Initiative {Website Plan}
1. Website Choice
Name:
Tech Bridge Youth Initiative
Organisation Description:
Tech Bridge Youth Initiative is a non-profit organisation that focuses on helping young people develop digital and technology skills. It supports youth, especially from disadvantaged communities, who lack access to proper technological education.
The organisation provides training in:
•	Computer literacy 
•	Coding 
•	Website development 
•	Digital marketing 
•	Basic IT support 
It also partners with schools and community centres to run workshops and training programmes.
Main Goal:
To bridge the digital divide and prepare young people for education, employment, and entrepreneurship in the modern digital world.











2. Reasons for Choosing the Organisation:
Technology is essential in today’s world, but many young people do not have access to digital training.
This website was chosen because it will:
•	Improve awareness of training opportunities
•	Allow easy online registration
•	Expand the organisation’s reach
•	Attract volunteers, sponsors, and partners
This makes the website a useful communication and development tool.

3. Website Goals and Objectives
Goals:
•	Inform users about the organisation and services 
•	Promote digital skills training programmes 
•	Support youth development through technology 
Objectives:
•	Provide clear and simple information 
•	Allow online registration for programmes and events 
•	Share news, events, and success stories 
•	Create a user-friendly, accessible design 
•	Ensure mobile and desktop compatibility
